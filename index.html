The Eight Sisters

Cultural Guide

Discover the heritage, landscapes, and spirit behind the music.

Assam

Brahmaputra & Tea

Home of the mighty Brahmaputra river, Kaziranga's one-horned rhinos, ancient temples, lush rolling tea gardens, and the rhythmic folk beats of Bihu.

Meghalaya

Abode of Clouds

Misty pine highlands, centuries-old Living Root Bridges woven from ficus elastica trees, crystal-clear Umngot waters in Dawki, and monsoon thunder.

Arunachal Pradesh

Dawn-Lit Mountains

Snow-capped Himalayan ridges, the spiritual calm of 400-year-old Tawang Monastery, and untouched ancient mountain passes.

Nagaland

Land of Festivals

Vibrant tribal heritage, ethereal lilies of Dzukou Valley, traditional log drums, folk polyphony, and the famous Hornbill celebration.

Manipur

Jeweled Land

The floating phumdi islands of Loktak Lake, birthplace of modern polo, classical Manipuri dance drama, and serene hillside valleys.

Mizoram

Songbird of the East

Clifftop bamboo forests, the rhythmic Cheraw bamboo dance, rolling green hills, and peaceful church-bell choirs echoing through the mist.

Sikkim

Himalayan Sanctuary

Guarded by sacred Mount Kangchenjunga, glacial alpine lakes like Tsomgo, rhododendron valleys, and peaceful Buddhist prayer flags.

Tripura

Royal Heritage

The marble splendor of Ujjayanta Palace, the water kingdom of Neermahal, and ancient mysterious rock-cut bas-reliefs at Unakoti.
